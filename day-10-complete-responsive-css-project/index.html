What is the purpose of this website?

It presents my skills, internship learning path, assignments and contact form.

Is the website responsive?

Yes. Its layout adjusts for mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Does the contact form send emails?

No. It uses browser validation only. A backend or form service is needed to send messages.

How can I test the website?
